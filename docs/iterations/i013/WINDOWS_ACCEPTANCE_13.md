# Windows sea trial: Iteration 13, The Catch & The Cove (0.101a)

A practical walkthrough for the Captain and Skipper, about 40 minutes: one
evening at sea without a harbour. Tick what works and note what does not,
ideally with a screenshot (Windows + Shift + S).

Nothing here has been tried on Windows yet. This trial is the acceptance.

**Get the project:** open the GitHub branch `claude/friendly-fermat-dey23f`,
choose **Code → Download ZIP**, extract it, open `project.godot` in Godot 4.7
and press **Run** (F5).

Tip: at least once, try a step with the **sound off**. Every bite, warning and
alarm should still be clear on screen.

## 1. The cat and the dog

- [ ] **New Game**: the preview shows the new illustrated cat; click the dog
  and see the new dog. Start with the cat.
- [ ] Go below. The cat looks like it belongs aboard (same drawn style as the
  boat and the sailor), at a sensible size next to the captain.
- [ ] Walk up to it: the **cat itself glows** (no circle) and the prompt says
  **Pet cat**. Press **E**: hearts, a purr, it sits up, then settles again.
- [ ] Stand at the chart table, the berth or the provisions with the cat
  nearby: the station keeps the E key; face the cat and step closer to pet it.
- [ ] Later (or in a second game) do the same with the **dog**.

## 2. Fishing while moored

- [ ] On deck in Copenhagen, find the **rod on the stern rail** (port side).
  Walking up makes the rod glow: **Fishing rod**.
- [ ] **E**: the panel says how and where you fish ("from the moored boat",
  Baltic). Choose the spinner, **Let the line out**.
- [ ] **Wait for a bite** (an hour at most per press). When a fish bites the
  fight starts: **hold E** to reel in, **let go** when the bar turns red
  ("Ease off!"). Land it.
- [ ] The result says what it is and that it is in the cool box. Try
  **Release** on one fish.
- [ ] Close the panel with the line out and let time run (5×): when a fish
  bites, a bell, **"Fish on!"** in the top-left, and a notice. Walk to the rod
  and press E within about 45 seconds.
- [ ] Wait too long once: the fish gets away, the line stays out.

## 3. The grocery store

- [ ] On the pontoon, the grocery store has **Fishing lures** (buy a soft bait
  or a jig) and **Sell the catch**.

## 4. Depart and troll

- [ ] Plan a short coastal route (Copenhagen → Malmö works well) and set sail.
- [ ] At the rod: "Trolling behind the boat at … kn". Let the line out. The boat
  keeps sailing; the helm still answers ("Check sail configuration").
- [ ] The HUD shows **Line out · trolling**. Wait for a bite; land it.
- [ ] If the boat goes faster than 7 kn the HUD says **too fast to troll**
  (amber) and a notice explains it.

## 5. All eight sailing directions

- [ ] Underway on deck the boat points along its course **in one of eight
  directions** (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW). Turns are smooth, never jittery.
- [ ] On a voyage with several legs (or by plotting new courses at the chart),
  see at least a straight and a diagonal direction.
- [ ] In each: **W walks up the screen, S down, A left, D right**. Walk to
  the rod, the windlass and the companionway: the glows and prompts match
  what you see.
- [ ] On the **Frogleap 36** or **Clam 40** sailing north or south, walk to the
  bow and the stern: the view follows you; you are never off-screen.
- [ ] Settings → **Sailing view** off: the boat stays bow-right underway.
  Turn it back on.

## 6. Find an anchorage and set the anchor

- [ ] Walk to the **windlass on the foredeck** (it glows: **Anchor**). Press E.
- [ ] Far from land the panel says it is too deep here. Near a coast it says
  how sheltered the spot is from the wind now (good / fair / exposed) and why.
  The panel also states the model's limits (no depth soundings).
- [ ] Choose **7 × the depth**, **Drop anchor**, and press E (or **Set it
  now**) as the marker crosses the green middle.
- [ ] The boat stops, the sails come down, the chain runs over the bow. The
  HUD says **At anchor · Holding well** (or fair); the route panel says
  "voyage paused".
- [ ] Press E at the helm: it asks you to raise the anchor first.

## 7. Cook the catch

- [ ] Go below to the **little stove** beside the provisions (it glows:
  **Cook a meal**).
- [ ] Choose **Pan-fried fish** or **Fish stew**: the panel shows what it needs
  and gives. **Cook**: a moment of sizzle and steam, then the result. At
  anchor with your own fish: **"A quiet evening at anchor. Fresh fish for
  supper."**
- [ ] The HUD says **Well fed**; the food stores went up.
- [ ] Underway in a fresh breeze (18 kn or more) the stove politely refuses.

## 8. Sleep at anchor, and the alarm

- [ ] Sleep 8 hours at a sheltered anchorage in steady weather: you sleep
  through.
- [ ] For the alarm: anchor somewhere exposed on short chain (3 ×) and wait for
  wind (or F9 → "Strong winds" in the editor). First the amber **Poor holding**,
  then if it gets worse the red **"Anchor dragging! Go to the windlass"**, a
  notice and a soft beep. If you were asleep, you are woken with the reason.
- [ ] Nothing terrible happens at once: the boat moves only a little; let out
  more chain or **Set it again**, or **Raise anchor**.

## 9. Raise the anchor and sail on

- [ ] **Raise anchor**: "Anchor up. The voyage continues." Sails go up, the
  boat sails on to its destination.

## 10. Trophies and the Captain's Log

- [ ] In the cabin, the **trophy board** on the wall shows little fish for your
  best catches. **E** lists the five best (rare and big first).
- [ ] **L** (Captain's Log): notable moments such as "Caught my first fish",
  "First anchorage of my own", "A quiet evening at anchor. Fresh fish for
  supper." Renown was paid once each, not for every fish.

## 11. Saving and loading along the way

- [ ] Save **with fish in the cool box**, load: the same fish, once.
- [ ] Save **at anchor**, quit to the menu, load: still at anchor in the same
  place, holding as before.
- [ ] Save **with the line out while trolling**, load: the line is out, the
  voyage continues.
- [ ] Load an **Iteration 12 save**: it opens normally, with the basic rod and
  two spinners.

## 12. Nothing else broke

- [ ] The gangplank, harbour services, Go Ashore, the hat (and its badge), the
  chart and the boatyard work as in Iteration 12, on all four boats.
